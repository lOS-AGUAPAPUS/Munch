ABOUT US

Sandwich Sandwich is a family owned Bristol business boasting over 30 years of experience preparing high quality food with big plans for the future. The art of running a successful sandwich shop is about making reasonably priced, high quality home made sandwiches.

Situated conveniently in locations across Bristol and London, we pride ourselves on making the tastiest sandwiches, so we use only the freshest and most delicious locally sourced ingredients to make our Sandwich Sandwiches just perfect.

Not quite sure what you would put in your perfect sandwich? Fear not! Pick from our range of our delicious, tried and tested house sandwiches or pick up one of our legendary Soft Boiled Scotch Eggs.

Catering for a meeting? Or just have no time to wait. Then please call ahead and we will have your order ready for you when you come to collect it.

Are you wondering where to go for lunch in Bristol? Sandwich Sandwich is the perfect place to get a lunchtime treat with friends to eat in or takeaway. You will soon see that our gourmet sandwiches are so good, they deserved to be named twice!
